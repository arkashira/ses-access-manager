# Customer Journey Map
## Aware

### Trigger Event
- **Event**: AWS SES production access request rejection
- **Frequency**: 1-2 times a month (estimated)
- **Source**: AWS SES dashboard or email notifications

### Friction Points
- **Lack of clear support**: Uncertainty about next steps or who to contact
- **Time-consuming manual process**: Manual requests and follow-ups with AWS support

### User Emotions
- **Frustration**: Repeated rejections and lack of clear guidance
- **Anxiety**: Uncertainty about meeting AWS SES requirements

### Opportunities to Delight
- **Clear and concise support documentation**: Easy-to-understand guides and FAQs
- **Proactive support escalation**: Automated tools for escalating issues to the right team

### Metric per Phase
- **Awareness**: 80% of customers are aware of AWS SES production access request rejections (estimated)

## Consider

### Trigger Event
- **Event**: Researching solutions for AWS SES production access request management
- **Frequency**: 1-2 times a quarter (estimated)
- **Source**: Online search, social media, or word of mouth

### Friction Points
- **Information overload**: Difficulty finding reliable and up-to-date information
- **Lack of trust**: Uncertainty about the effectiveness and security of third-party solutions

### User Emotions
- **Confusion**: Difficulty understanding the problem and potential solutions
- **Skepticism**: Uncertainty about the credibility and expertise of third-party providers

### Opportunities to Delight
- **Comprehensive and accurate information**: Easy-to-understand guides, case studies, and testimonials
- **Expertise and credibility**: Demonstrated expertise and credibility through case studies, certifications, and industry recognition

### Metric per Phase
- **Consideration**: 20% of customers consider third-party solutions for AWS SES production access request management (estimated)
