# Marketing Plan for ses-access-manager
## Positioning
### Primary Positioning Statement
"Simplify AWS SES production access management for small/mid-size businesses and ISPs with automated request tools and support escalation."

### Alternative Positioning Statements
1. "Streamline AWS SES access requests and resolve support issues with ease."
2. "Unlock seamless AWS SES production access for your business with our automated solution."
3. "Say goodbye to AWS SES access headaches with our intuitive request management tool."
